How to consolidate items into to-dos:

| **Tool**             | **How it works**                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Email**            | Every Trello board has a unique email address. You can **forward any email** to that address, and Trello automatically turns the subject line into a card title and the body into the description. |
| **Slack / Teams**    | You can use the Trello integration to **turn a chat message into a card** with two clicks. You don't have to copy-paste; you just select "Add Card" from the message options.                      |
| **Chrome Extension** | If you are on a website you want to save, you click the extension icon. It **grabs the URL and page title automatically** and lets you sent it to a board instantly.                               |
| **Mobile App**       | This refers to using the **"Share" menu** on your phone. If you're looking at a photo or a link in another app, you hit the share button and select Trello to "capture" it.                        |
